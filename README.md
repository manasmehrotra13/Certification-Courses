A simulation with 4 tasks, for a made-up lender called Geldium:

1. Explore a dataset of about 500 customers and profile who is likely to miss payments
2. Plan how to predict missed payments with AI
3. Write a business report and tell the story behind the data, for a collections strategy
4. Present an AI-driven collections strategy

## Accenture: Data Analytics and Visualization (Nov 2024)

The made-up client, Social Buzz, is a social media company. The task was to find its top 5 content categories.

- I cleaned and joined 3 tables (reactions, content and reaction types) into one table of 24,573 rows
- I built pivot tables and charts from it
- I presented the findings in an 11-slide deck, using Forage's template

**What I found:** there are 16 content categories. Animals and Science are the most popular. Food themes like Healthy Eating also rank high, so working with healthy-eating brands could lift engagement.
